Tonepath

Free jazz improvisation practice in the browser. Type a chord progression, and Tonepath gives each chord its scale, laid out as a hexagonal note grid: the next step of the scale to the right, a third up and to the left, every note coloured by its degree.

  • Write etude: write a melody over the changes by picking notes on the grid, shown as notation as you go.
  • Read etude: play the etude from one page, with a click and a moving playhead.
  • Improvise: play over the chord chart with a click, the grid following each chord as it comes.
  • Tune: warm up and tune; the grid lights every note you play into the microphone.

No account and no backend: progressions are saved in your browser, with export and import. Built by Truelathe; more about it on the Tonepath home page; the code is open source on GitHub.
