## Question

What should Practice **tell the player** beyond the live count, targets and the flash when the form moves on? E.g.
per slot: notes played vs. the count, whether the change was made on a target, wrong notes in Follow; per pass
through the form: a summary (slots landed on a guide tone, misses), kept or thrown away; shown where (the chart's
slots, the practice panel, an end-of-pass card). Decide what is measured, where it is shown, and whether anything is
saved.
