## Question

Can colour-blind players tell chord (green) / scale (blue) / passing (brown) / grey apart — on note circles, on piano-strip keys (tints and black-key fills), and with out-of-range notes drawn hollow and faded ([Instrument view](011-instrument-view.md))? Check the palette against common colour-vision deficiencies and decide on a palette tweak and/or a secondary cue (shape, outline weight, ring, glyph), plus contrast of the white letter on each fill and in dark/light themes if any. Prototype the candidates side by side.

## Resolution

**Out of scope / superseded by the etude pivot.** The green / blue / brown / grey role colours (and piano-strip key tints) no longer exist. Colour now means **degree borders (1–7)** plus **white/black hex fills** (chord vs in-scale non-chord). Replaced by **Colour accessibility (etude)**.
