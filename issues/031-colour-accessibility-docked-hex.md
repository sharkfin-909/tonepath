## Question

Can colour-blind players tell **degree borders (1–7, thicker stroke)** plus **white vs black fills** apart on the range-spanning docked hex, including grey out-of-range cells and letter contrast? Check CVD; decide palette tweaks and/or secondary cues. Prototype side by side using [Docked hex grid](027-docked-hex-grid.md). Replaces [Colour accessibility (etude)](024-colour-accessibility-etude.md). Since [Docked hex grid](027-docked-hex-grid.md), each cell also shows a **degree number** (a non-colour cue) and the name without octave; there are still only 7 border colours. Also check that the play-time same-pitch highlight reads clearly on white, black and grey cells.
