PROTOTYPERound 4 — one column per octave, no octave numbers on notes (faint numbers at the foot of each column are the octave)
Click a slot to target it (or [ / ]). Add notes by typing (a–g focuses the box: e4, d5 f5 a5) + Enter, or click the piano strip — a key toggles that pitch in the slot (dot = already there). Click a note to hear it and select it; ↑/↓ moves its pitch (Shift = octave); Backspace deletes. Click a chord label to type a chord (Tab = next slot; Dm7 G7 splits a bar).
